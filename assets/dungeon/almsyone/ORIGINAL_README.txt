LOW POLY FANTASY DUNGEON KIT  v1.0
==================================

87 game-ready low-poly models: a modular dungeon kit + props + a Halloween set.
FREE / pay what you want.

MODULAR KIT (23)  - snaps on a 2 m grid, walls 3 m tall
  Floors: Stone, Stone_Cracked, Stone_Mossy, Wood, Trapdoor, SpikeTrap
  Walls: Wall, Wall_Mossy, Wall_Cracked, Wall_Half, Wall_Doorway, Wall_Window,
         Wall_Arch, Wall_CellBars, Wall_Corner
  Pillar, Pillar_Broken, Stairs, Rubble, Door_Wood, Door_Cell, Portcullis

PROPS (52)
  Treasure: Chest_Closed, Chest_Open, Mimic, Gold_Pile, Coins, Gem, Key, Potions x3
  Storage: Barrel, Barrel_Broken, Crate, Crate_Small, Sack
  Lighting: Torch_Wall, Torch_Standing, Brazier, Candles, Candelabra
  Weapons: Sword, Shield, Axe, Spear, WeaponRack
  Furniture: Table, Chair, Stool, Bench, Bookshelf, Book_Stack, Book_Open, Scroll, Shelf_Wall
  Big pieces: Throne, Altar, Sarcophagus, Statue_Knight, Fountain, Well
  Dark: Skull, Bones_Pile, Skeleton_Sitting, Chains_Wall, Cage, Cobweb, Lever
  Magic/decor: Cauldron, Crystals_Blue, Crystals_Purple, Banner_Red, Banner_Blue, Rug

HALLOWEEN SET (12)
  Pumpkin, JackOLantern, Pumpkin_Pile, Tombstone_Round, Tombstone_Cross,
  Tombstone_Broken, Coffin, Lantern_Spooky, Skull_Candle, Ghost, Bat, WitchHat

SPECS
  ~22,700 triangles total
  Real-world scale (metres)
  Modular pieces: pivot at grid centre / wall centre-line, floor top at z=0
  Props: pivot bottom-centre. Wall-mounted items (torch, banner, chains,
  lever, shelf, spooky lantern, cobweb): pivot at the wall/hook point
  Flat-shaded, no textures: shared solid-colour materials
  Fire, crystals, potions, jack-o'-lantern faces use emissive materials

FORMATS
  FBX/  - Unity, Unreal, Godot (scale applied, Y-up)
  GLB/  - Godot, Three.js, Babylon, web viewers
  OBJ/  - universal (with .mtl)
  LowPolyDungeonKit.blend - source (Blender 5.1) incl. showcase dungeon
