# AlmsyONE puzzle chamber

Only SupplyChamber uses PuzzleChamber.tscn. Its original 14 x 14 m bounds,
floor/wall collisions and north/east openings are generated by the unchanged
dungeon_region.gd. HiddenDoor, SecretPassage, HiddenRewardRoom, RewardChest,
pickups and all puzzle/interaction logic remain unchanged.

Eight unchanged GLBs are added: SM_Altar, SM_Crystals_Blue,
SM_Crystals_Purple, SM_Book_Open, SM_Scroll, SM_Bookshelf, SM_Candelabra and
SM_Candles. Stone material instances use RGB x 0.40, matching previous rooms.
Decorative crystal emission is reduced to 0.12 so blue/purple remain visible
under existing lighting. Rune crystals still follow original rune emission.
The original source files and license are preserved.

The three RuneSwitch bodies keep the exact existing 0.65 m radius / 0.7 m high
cylinder collision, world positions, interaction distance, Glyph, RuneMesh and
Glow. Only the old Pedestal mesh is hidden. RuneAltarVisual fits the new altar
inside that cylinder (1.10 x 0.60 m footprint; 0.70 m overall height).
Its per-rune crystal material follows the original RuneMesh color/emission for
idle, correct activation, wrong order and reset. This follower never activates
a rune or changes sequence, progress, cooldowns, signals, inventory or doors.

Room decorations add no collision, lights, navigation or interactions. Reading
material and candles rest on the side altar's 1 m tabletop. The original clue
and status labels remain the only puzzle instructions. Props leave all three
rune approaches, pickup locations and the hidden-door path clear. Wall/decor
geometry uses single-surface meshes under existing camera occluders.
